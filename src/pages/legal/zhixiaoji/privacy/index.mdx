Zhixiaoji respects your personal information and privacy. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the app to understand how information may be processed and how you can manage it.

If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please stop using Zhixiaoji.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the compact thermal printing features provided by Zhixiaoji, including device connection, content editing, image and document import, web printing, print preview, print history, local drafts, and app settings.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, third-party services, third-party file sources, or external webpages that you open through Zhixiaoji. Their information practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We May Process

Zhixiaoji currently focuses on local printing workflows. To provide device connection, editing, and printing features, the app may process the following information on your device.

### 2.1 Device and Connection Information

When you connect a printer, Zhixiaoji may process nearby Bluetooth device names, device identifiers, connection status, service characteristics, paper or consumable status, printer responses, connection errors, and print job status.

This information is used to discover devices, establish connections, select available channels, send print data, determine print status, and diagnose connection problems.

### 2.2 Content You Create or Import

You may create, import, or edit text, images, documents, web content, templates, labels, drafts, and print preview content in Zhixiaoji.

This content is usually processed locally on your device for editing, previewing, generating print data, and printing again. You are responsible for confirming the legality and backup needs of your content.

### 2.3 Local Drafts, History, and Cache

To improve user experience, Zhixiaoji may store drafts, templates, print history, recently used devices, app preferences, preview cache, image processing cache, or temporary files locally on your device.

Deleting the app, clearing app data, changing devices, or system abnormalities may cause this local data to be lost.

### 2.4 Logs and Error Information

To locate problems and improve stability, Zhixiaoji may record device connection errors, print failure reasons, app crashes, or abnormal states locally. If a future version provides a user feedback feature, you may choose to include relevant logs when submitting feedback.

## 3. System Permissions

Zhixiaoji requests system permissions only when they are needed for specific features. Permission names may vary by operating system version. The system prompt on your device prevails.

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bluetooth | Discover, connect to, and manage Bluetooth printers; send print jobs; read printer status. | Required for Bluetooth printing |
| Location-related permission | Some Android systems associate Bluetooth scanning with location permission. It is used to discover nearby Bluetooth devices. Zhixiaoji does not use this permission to continuously track your location. | Required for Bluetooth scanning on some systems |
| Camera | Capture or import images for printing. | Required only when using camera capture |
| Photos or media library | Select, import, edit, and print images. | Required only when importing images |
| File access | Select, read, preview, or print files that you actively import. | Required only when importing files |
| Network access | Open webpages you actively visit, load webpage content, check necessary resources, or support future service capabilities. | Required only for related network features |

You can manage these permissions in system settings. Disabling a permission may make related features unavailable, but it does not affect features that do not depend on that permission.

## 4. How We Use Information

We process relevant information mainly to:

1. Discover, connect to, and manage printing devices.
2. Edit, preview, convert, and send printable content.
3. Save local drafts, templates, history records, and preferences.
4. Identify connection exceptions, print failures, or compatibility problems.
5. Improve app stability, compatibility, and user experience.
6. Comply with legal requirements or protect the lawful rights and interests of users, developers, and third parties.

## 5. Storage

Zhixiaoji's drafts, history, templates, cache, and preference information are usually stored locally on your device. We recommend that you back up important content yourself.

If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, online templates, remote backup, user feedback, or other online services, we will update this Privacy Policy before those features are launched and obtain your authorization or consent where necessary.

## 6. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We do not actively sell your personal information.

We may process or disclose necessary information in the following situations:

1. We have obtained your explicit consent.
2. It is necessary to implement a feature that you actively choose, such as calling system capabilities, printer devices, or third-party services.
3. It is required by laws, regulations, administrative authorities, or judicial authorities.
4. It is necessary to protect the safety and lawful rights of users, developers, third parties, or the public interest.
5. It is necessary for a merger, division, acquisition, asset transfer, or similar transaction, in which case we will require the new holder to continue to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Third-Party SDKs and Open-Source Components

Zhixiaoji may use third-party or open-source components to implement Bluetooth connection, file selection, image processing, web display, document preview, permission requests, or basic runtime capabilities.

These components may process necessary data locally according to their functions. If a future version integrates a third-party SDK that independently collects personal information, we will supplement the policy or list with its name, purpose, processing method, and privacy policy link.

## 8. Web Printing and Cookies

When you use web printing or built-in web browsing capabilities, target webpages may use Cookies, LocalStorage, scripts, advertisements, or other technologies.

The processing of information by third-party webpages is the responsibility of the corresponding webpage or service provider. You should decide whether to access, log in, or submit information yourself. Zhixiaoji does not recommend entering sensitive information on untrusted webpages.

## 9. Your Rights

You can manage your information and permissions by:

1. Deleting drafts, history, templates, or cache in the app, subject to the features provided by the current version.
2. Disabling Bluetooth, camera, photos, file, or location-related permissions in system settings.
3. Deleting locally stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.
4. Contacting us through the information in this policy for questions, feedback, or complaints.

If future versions provide accounts, cloud sync, or online services, we will provide access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, account deletion, or related contact methods as required by applicable laws and regulations.

## 10. Minors

If you are a minor, you should use Zhixiaoji with the consent and guidance of your guardian. Guardians should help minors understand this Privacy Policy and guide them in using printing, importing, and web access features properly.

## 11. Information Security

We will take reasonable technical and management measures to protect information security, such as limiting unnecessary data processing, prioritizing local processing, controlling permission requests, and reducing unnecessary retention.

However, the internet environment, terminal systems, and Bluetooth connections are not absolutely secure. You should properly protect your device, system permissions, files, and print content, and avoid processing sensitive content in untrusted environments.

## 12. Cross-Border Processing

Zhixiaoji currently processes printing-related information mainly on your device. If future online services, cloud sync, technical support, or other features involve cross-border processing, we will comply with applicable notice, authorization, and security requirements.

## 13. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy based on product features, laws, regulations, or business changes. The updated policy will be displayed through the app, website, or other reasonable methods.

If an update materially changes how personal information is processed, we will provide a prominent notice. If you continue using Zhixiaoji after the policy is updated, you are deemed to have accepted the updated policy.
